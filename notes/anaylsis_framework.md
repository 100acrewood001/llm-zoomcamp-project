## ✅ **Annual Report Analysis Cheat Sheet**

### **1. Start with the Big Picture**
- **CEO Letter & Management Discussion (MD&A)**  
  - Look for strategy, risks, and future outlook.
  - Compare tone vs. actual numbers (overly optimistic?).

---

### **2. Key Financial Statements**
#### **Income Statement**
- Revenue growth trend (YoY, QoQ).
- Gross margin, operating margin, net margin.
- EPS (Earnings Per Share) trend.

#### **Balance Sheet**
- **Liquidity**: Current Ratio = Current Assets ÷ Current Liabilities.
- **Leverage**: Debt-to-Equity Ratio.
- Asset growth vs. liabilities.

#### **Cash Flow Statement**
- **Operating Cash Flow**: Positive and growing?
- **Free Cash Flow** = Operating CF – CapEx.
- Compare Net Income vs. Cash Flow (earnings quality).

---

### **3. Ratios That Matter**
- **Profitability**: ROE, ROA.
- **Efficiency**: Asset Turnover.
- **Valuation**: P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA.

---

### **4. Red Flags**
- Revenue ↑ but cash flow ↓.
- Frequent “one-time” adjustments.
- Rising debt without revenue growth.
- Aggressive accounting (check *Financial Shenanigans* techniques).

---

### **5. Notes & Footnotes**
- Contingent liabilities.
- Revenue recognition policies.
- Related-party transactions.

---

### **6. Compare & Contextualize**
- Benchmark vs. peers and industry.
- Check historical trends (3–5 years).
- Read analyst commentary (e.g., Seeking Alpha, Bloomberg).

---

### **7. Qualitative Factors**
- Management credibility.
- Competitive advantage (moat).
- Regulatory or macroeconomic risks.

---

**Pro Tip:** Use this flow →  
**Narrative → Numbers → Ratios → Risks → Context → Conclusion.**
